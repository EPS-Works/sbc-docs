## Enclosures

If you want to speed up your integration process, we offer three enclosures with different characteristics and IP rating.

| Image | Description | Material | Applications | IP rating |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ![ip52](img/Cinch_IP52.png) | Standard wiring, 3 LEDs, up to 4 SMA, Front panel can be customized with legend, client logo, etc. | Plastic | Indoor | IP52 |
| ![ip67](img/Cinch_IP67.png) | Special wiring, no LEDs, up to 2 SMA. | Plastic | Indoor / Outdoor | IP67 |
| ![ip68](img/Bopla_IP68.png) | Front panel and wiring can be completely customized, multiple accessories available (impact protection, wall brackets, etc.). | Aluminium | Indoor / Outdoor | IP68 |
